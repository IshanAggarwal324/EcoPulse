# EcoPulse Billing & Usage

## How is my energy usage measured?

EcoPulse measures consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh). Every reading from your connected nodes and devices is recorded with a timestamp, the node it came from, and its source (IoT device, simulator, or public grid feed). Your usage is the sum of consumed kWh over the period you select — for example the last 7 days or the current billing cycle.

## How is a billing period defined?

A billing period is a rolling window of your choosing. The Dashboard and Report pages default to a 7-day view, but you can compare the current period against the previous one. The assistant reports two numbers together: your total consumed kWh this period and your prior period total, then the percentage change so you can see whether usage went up or down.

## What does the bill analysis tell me?

When you ask about your bill, the assistant pulls a structured summary that includes your total consumption, the change versus the previous period, your top energy-consuming nodes, and any anomaly flags it detected. Anomaly flags highlight sudden spikes — for example a node consuming far more than its usual baseline — so you can investigate a device that may be malfunctioning or running unexpectedly.

## Why does my usage look different this period?

Usage can change because of weather, new devices, longer billing windows, or a node going offline and missing readings. If readings are missing for part of the period, the totals reflect only what was actually received, and the assistant will note that data may be incomplete rather than estimating a number.

## How accurate are the numbers?

All billing and usage numbers come directly from recorded readings. The assistant is instructed never to invent or estimate figures. If data for the requested period is unavailable, it will tell you so instead of guessing.
